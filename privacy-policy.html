This Privacy Policy explains what information CAC Good Works Assembly Believers Bible College ("the College," "we," "us") collects through this e-learning platform, how it's used, and how it's protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you use this platform as a Student, Teacher, or Administrator, we collect:

  • Account information: full name, email address, and a College-issued ID and passcode.
  • Academic records: course enrollment, attendance, exam results, grades, and certificates.
  • Content you submit: exam answers, questions asked to teachers, feedback, and any files uploaded (for Teachers/Admins).
  • Technical information: device type, browser type, and approximate usage times, used for attendance records and security logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate your account and give you access to the correct courses and materials.
  • To record attendance, grade exams, and issue certificates.
  • To allow Teachers to communicate with their students (answering questions, posting announcements).
  • To send account and academic notifications by email, where enabled.
  • To maintain a security/activity log for administrative oversight.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

This platform is built on free-tier services provided by other companies, who process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, and optional file storage)
  • Google Drive (optional file storage, when a Teacher/Admin chooses it)
  • Google Translate (on-demand translation of reading materials and exam questions — only the text being translated at that moment is sent)
  • EmailJS (sending account and academic notification emails, where enabled)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

Academic records (attendance, results, certificates) are retained for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping purposes, consistent with the College's academic policies.

5. Your Rights

You may request to review, correct, or delete your personal information by contacting the College Administrator using the details below. Account deactivation and data removal are handled through the Admin dashboard.

6. Children's Privacy

This platform is intended for Bible college students, teachers, and staff. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

7. Security

We use industry-standard authentication and encrypted connections (HTTPS) throughout. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we take reasonable steps to protect your information, including role-based access control and activity logging.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
